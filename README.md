## 📖 About the Comic

**The Ancient Well** is an original comic project that brings together storytelling, character design, and AI-assisted visual creation.

The comic explores a mysterious story centered around an ancient well, inviting readers into a world of secrets, discoveries, and unanswered questions.

## 📚 Story

The story of **The Ancient Well** is entirely original, created specifically for this comic. Its narrative, plot, and dialogue are independently written to bring the world and its characters to life.

Every conversation and event is part of the original creative vision behind the project.

## 🎨 Character Design

All character designs are original and enhanced by AI**.

The characters have their own visual identities and designs, developed to match the atmosphere and story of the comic.

## 🤖 Artwork & AI Generation

The comic pages are generated using **ChatGPT**.

AI-assisted generation is used to transform the original story, dialogue, and character designs into visual comic pages.

The creative concept, original story, dialogue, and character designs are part of the project's original creative work.
